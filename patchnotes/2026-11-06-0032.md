### MyDirtyHoe · `2.3.4` → `2.3.7`

<sub>by **MagicMike** · [Thunderstore](https://thunderstore.io/c/valheim/p/MagicMike/MyDirtyHoe/) · released 5 Nov 2026</sub>

#### 2.3.7

- With the Dirty Hoe in hand, holding Alt no longer makes your character sit down, so the Undo Hotkey (Left Alt + X) doesn't sit you down too.
- The placement labels of the river, road and ramp tools are smaller, and keep about the same size on screen near or far, instead of filling the view up close.
- The River piece no longer costs Stone, even with Enable Resource Costs on.
- (Experimental): Waterfalls. A stretch of a river that falls at least 1 m, more steeply than the Max Slope, becomes a waterfall instead of being refused: the river runs on to the cliff's edge and pours over it, carried in an arc that clears a sheer cliff and runs down over a slope, into a pool dug where it lands. The brink is worn down for the water, and a channel is cut for it down a slope.
- Waterfalls have their own shader: white water racing down, fraying at the sides, with spray off the lip, splashes, mist and foam where it lands, foamy rapids above the edge, and the sound of falling water. The river and the fall blend into each other at the top, and the fall into the pool at the bottom.
- Boats and swimmers carried over the edge drop into the pool. New settings: Max Waterfall Height (the tallest waterfall the river tool builds) and Waterfall Pool Foam (how solid the foam on the pool is).
- Rivers blend into the sea: where a river comes down to sea level, its water fades into the sea's, and no banks or levees are built in the water.
- The waterfall sounds are Nox_Sound's "Ambiance_Waterfall_Small_Close_Loop_Stereo" and "Ambiance_Waterfall_Big_Skogafoss_Loop_Stereo" (CC0, Freesound).

#### 2.3.6

- (Experimental): New River piece for the hoe. Click points from a river's source to its mouth, then click the last point again: the river is dug through them and filled with water you can swim in and float boats and rafts on.
- The water slopes with the river and only ever runs downhill from the first point. Banks rise to the ground, and are built up into levees where the river crosses a dip, so the water is held in.
- Scroll to change the width, and hold Left Shift and scroll to change the depth. The preview shows the course, the water's edge, the banks, and how far the water falls.
- Rivers are saved with the world and shared in multiplayer.
- The current carries boats, rafts, swimmers and floating items downstream, faster on sloping stretches and slower near the banks; paddling and swimming upstream are harder. Set with the new River Current setting (0 turns it off).
- Rivers sound like running water. The sound follows you along the river from the nearest place on it and fades out about 40 m away. Set with the new River Sound Volume setting (0 turns it off). The recording is DanGasior's "Rapid Mountain River Close" (CC0, Freesound).
- Rivers have their own flowing water: ripples that run downstream, faster on steep stretches, soft foam along the banks and in fast water, sun glints and reflections of the sky. The shader and textures were made for MyDirtyHoe.
- Aim at a river's water and press Delete twice to remove the whole river and put back the ground it changed. Undoing a river just built takes away its water too.
- New River Settings in the config: Enable River Tool, River Width, River Depth, Max Slope, River Paint, River Depth Modifier, Remove River Key, River Current and River Sound Volume.
- The mod's assets now load from memory, which fixes a "file is corrupted" error on start-up with the updated asset bundle.

#### 2.3.5

- Clear Forestry and the remove_forestry console command now remove the very large rocks along the shore. A big rock is removed when any part of it is inside the circle, not only its middle.
- They also remove rocks that have already been hit and broken apart, and ore rocks such as tin and obsidian.
- They also clear the small pebbles on the ground. The pebbles are drawn by the game like grass, so the cleared area is remembered with the world and shared in multiplayer.
- The terrain reset (the Reset Terrain piece, the reset modifier key, the Reset Hotkey and the resetterrain console command) brings the pebbles back where it touches a cleared area. Undo doesn't bring cleared pebbles back.

### WeatherOverhaul · `1.0.2` → `1.0.5`

<sub>by **Ivvty** · [Hexium](https://valheim.hexium.gg/mods/Ivvty/WeatherOverhaul) · released 4 Nov 2026</sub>

#### 1.0.5

- Lightning lights the ground and the fog where it strikes, even far away.
- No more bars of light across a near bolt where it bends.
- The moon's phase keys no longer flood a server that refuses them (such as one with an anticheat): each player asks for a key once a night instead of every two seconds.

#### 1.0.4

- The sound no longer stutters, repeating its last moment, when the game briefly pauses to clean up memory (in any weather, even while paused; most noticed with large mod lists). The thunder is as loud as before.

#### 1.0.3

- Lightning can now strike close by (rarely), hitting roofs and treetops.
- Players in one thunderstorm see and hear the same lightning.
- Clouds no longer jitter on small mouse movements, far from the world's centre or with first-person mods.
- New settings to adjust each kind of sky and each weather: cloud cover, cloud and sky brightness, visibility, the light on the land and its colour.
- New setting for how many clear-weather days have broken clouds.
- The moon now hides the stars behind it.
