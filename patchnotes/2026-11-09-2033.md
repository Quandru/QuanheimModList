## Patch notes

### FarLands · `1.2.0` → `1.3.0`

<sub>by **Ivvty** · [Hexium](https://valheim.hexium.gg/mods/Ivvty/FarLands) · released 8 Nov 2026</sub>

- Changed: the grass far off is now the game's own grass. The grain and patches painted on the land (`Detail.FarGrass`), which
  from the ground looked like shading on bare land, are gone. The game's grass now reaches 200 m instead of
  about 45 m, reeds and water lilies apart (`Detail.GrassDistance`, 0-300 m; 0 leaves it to the game), the same grass in the same places, as far as the
  loaded land goes. It needs compute shaders; without them the game's radius stays.
- It is drawn on the graphics card and built on a background thread, without a game object for each patch: 200 m against
  45 m costs at most about 0.4 ms of main thread and 0.3 ms of GPU a frame on the test machine, walking or standing. A
  rebuilt patch keeps its old grass until the new one is ready. Where another mod patches the game's grass build, patches
  are left to the game's build and the log names it.
- The GPU grass was SteadyFrame's; the same code is now in FarLands, and with both installed one of them runs it. Next to a
  SteadyFrame older than 1.3.0 the grass radius stays the game's: update SteadyFrame too.
- Added: more of the world's lights glow far off at night: the torches of the Ashlands' castles and the giants' halls,
  the dvergr's lanterns and mist lamps (the fulings' fires and the traders' fire pits glowed already).
- Added: the green pillar over the bell fragments in the Ashlands' fortresses is seen far off, by day and by night,
  where the game stops drawing it with its zone.
- Fixed: with More Vanilla Build Prefabs (or another mod that makes the game's objects buildable), FarLands took every
  tree, bush, rock and pickable of the world for a building: small trees far off swayed from the root in the wind, and
  the far buildings ran out of room, so bases could vanish from the far view. The world's own buildings are now known by
  what the game wears down (their walls), not by what a mod lets you build.
- Fixed: stone fences and fence gates no longer vanish far off long before the rest of a base.
- Fixed: the fulings' straw roofs, the Ashlands' charred banners, and banners and cloth doors on your bases are drawn far
  off, held still (they were left out, or swayed from the wrong point).

### QuanChunkLoader · `1.1.2` → `1.1.3`

<sub>by **Quandru** · [Thunderstore](https://thunderstore.io/c/valheim/p/Quandru/QuanChunkLoader/) · released 8 Nov 2026</sub>

- Pressing Shift while typing (in chat, the console, a sign or a menu) no longer shows the zone grid of the observer

- The "had to be created again" warning no longer counts things that move (creatures, players, birds, fish, items,

  player at the base. It now counts only things that stay put, such as buildings, stations, containers and plants.

### SteadyFrame · `1.2.2` → `1.3.0`

<sub>by **Ivvty** · [Hexium](https://valheim.hexium.gg/mods/Ivvty/SteadyFrame) · released 8 Nov 2026</sub>

- Grass (Draw on the GPU): the grass of each patch is built on a background thread and goes straight to the graphics card,
  without a game object for it, and the game's per-frame pass over the grass visits only what changed. Grass to 200 m costs
  about 0.2 ms of main thread less a frame while walking, and a rebuilt patch keeps its old grass until the new one is ready.
  It stays the game's build where another mod patches that build (named in the log) and during the picture check.
- The GPU grass code is now shared with FarLands 1.3.0, which draws the game's grass to 200 m with it; with both installed
  one of them runs it. Update both: a FarLands 1.3.0 next to an older SteadyFrame keeps the game's grass radius.
- The DLL now tells BepInEx its real version (1.2.2 still said 1.2.1).
