## Highlights

### 🌱 Planters take more plants

**QuanPieces** planters now accept everything you plant with the cultivator, not just crop and tree seedlings. PlantEverything's mushrooms, berry bushes and flowers can now go in them.

### 🧹 Cleaner planter removal

Removing a **QuanPieces** planter now clears everything in it. Anything ready to pick drops its harvest, and PlantEverything's mushrooms and bushes give back what PlantEverything gives when removed by hand. Fully grown crops drop their harvest instead of falling unpicked, and plants no longer get left floating in the air.

### 🔧 Cultivator tint fix

Aiming the cultivator at a planter's soil no longer tints the whole planter blue. Aiming at the rim still does.
