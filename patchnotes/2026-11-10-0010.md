## Patch notes

### FarLands · `1.3.0` → `1.4.1`

<sub>by **Ivvty** · [Hexium](https://valheim.hexium.gg/mods/Ivvty/FarLands) · released 9 Nov 2026</sub>

#### 1.4.1

- Fixed: in or near a base with many fires and lamps, each far light whose reach was outside the view was still
  worked out over the whole screen. At a base of 4800 pieces that took about 2 ms of graphics card time every frame; now
  such a light costs nothing. The lights look the same.
- Changed: slightly less work each frame for the far fires and lamps, the far tree models and the rocks.

#### 1.4.0

- Fixed: PortalPreview's views had no grass while FarLands built it in the background. With PortalPreview 1.5.5 it is in the
  views saved from now on.
- Changed: the green pillar over the bell fragments in the Ashlands' fortresses now fades out between 1000 and 1500 m. It
  was drawn out to the buildings' distance at full strength, because nothing dims it with distance, and stood over
  everything from far outside the Ashlands. The new setting `Buildings.BeaconDistance` sets where it ends; 0 leaves only
  the game's own, close by.
- Fixed: `Detail.GrassDistance` at 0 now really leaves the grass to the game. FarLands kept drawing it on the graphics
  card and following its edge; only the radius went back.
- Fixed: next to a mod that sets the grass radius itself, such as HD Terrain, FarLands wrote its own back every second
  and all the grass was rebuilt each time. It now leaves the radius to that mod for the world and says so in the log.

- Fixed: the grass radius has one owner among the mods that set it: Valheim Visuals while its own radius setting is on,
  then FarLands, then the game. Switching one of them off in the settings could leave the other's radius, or the game's,
  in place of the one that should now hold.
- Fixed: when the background build of the grass and the game's own build changed places, all the grass was rebuilt at
  once and much of it was missing for a moment. Now the patches change over without a gap.
- Fixed: with SteadyFrame installed but switched off at start, FarLands now runs the shared GPU grass itself.

### QuanChunkLoader · `1.1.3` → `1.2.0`

<sub>by **Quandru** · [Hexium](https://valheim.hexium.gg/mods/Quandru/QuanChunkLoader) · released 10 Nov 2026</sub>

- **Eitr radiation pauses while nobody is near.** Dropped Refined Eitr and running eitr refineries fire radiation
  bolts (30 lightning + 30 poison, in random directions) on whichever machine runs them. In a loaded base with nobody
  there, the server ran them all night: on 9 Nov 2026 a pile of over 100 dropped Eitr wrecked much of a base. Now,
  in loaded zones with no player near, they hold their fire, as if the zone were unloaded. A player arriving brings
  back the vanilla sparks and damage. New section `8 - Unattended bases`, "Pause Eitr radiation while nobody is
  near", on by default. The 5-minute summary counts the bolts held back.
- **New section `9 - Known error suppression (personal)`**, with one switch so far, "Vanilla default model collider
  no read access" (off by default). On, it stops the repeated "RuntimeNavMeshBuilder: Source mesh default is skipped
  because it does not allow read access" lines. They come from about 800 vanilla models (cart wheels, ship hulls,
  logs, stones) the navmesh build can't read and skips anyway; a loaded base rebuilds its navmesh far more often, so
  it logs far more of them. This leaves those models out before the build, so creatures path exactly as before.
- **The log says what it knows, once per session**, for whoever reads it (you or an AI): when that warning appears
  with the switch off, a line names it as harmless and points to the switch; with the switch on, a line says the
  warnings are being suppressed and by which setting.
- Both are applied on their own: if a game update breaks either, the log says so and only that feature turns off.
