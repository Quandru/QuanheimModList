### FarLands · `1.1.1` → `1.2.0`

<sub>by **Ivvty** · [Hexium](https://valheim.hexium.gg/mods/Ivvty/FarLands) · released 8 Nov 2026</sub>

#### 1.2.0

- Added: where the game's grass thins out, out to the horizon, the Meadows and Plains have the grain of grass, and the
  Plains their red flowers, in the same places the game would grow them. It starts on the game's own land near you, so
  no bare ground lies between its grass and the far land, and follows the grass radius if another mod changes it. It
  costs no main thread and some 0.03-0.1 ms of GPU (2560x1440). Off with `Detail.FarGrass`.
- Changed: the game's grass no longer goes over every patch around you each frame to find the few that came into reach
  or left it; only the edge of its radius is looked at. The grass is the same, frame for frame. At the game's 45 m it
  saves next to nothing (0.01 of 0.02 ms); with the grass raised to 200 m (ValheimVisuals with SteadyFrame, or another mod) it
  takes 0.03 ms a frame where the game took 0.4. With CommunityPatchExtras' Grass Distance raised it builds as many patches
  a frame as that mod does: 0.02 ms where the game took 0.11 at its 128 m. The same code is in SteadyFrame; with both
  installed one of them runs it.
- Fixed: far fires and lamps with the sky behind them (on a tower, a hill) were dimmed by the clouds and could be lost.
- Changed: a far bonfire now has the size of the game's own flame, so you can steer by it, and burns as a moving fire out
  to some 500 m instead of a smooth spot.
- Changed: thick fog now hides far lights: they hold their brightness through thin haze and then fade fast, as the air
  around them does, where they used to shine through almost anything.
- Fixed: a far fire partly behind something flickered on and off with the game's anti-aliasing. A far bonfire is now cut by
  what stands in front of it, and a far spot of light dims by how much of it is hidden.
- Added: far fires leave a broken red streak on the water under them, as the game's own do up close. It fades with
  distance and with the air: faint at 800 m on a clear night, gone by 1500 m.
- Added: the world's own buildings (ruins, towers, houses, the walls of the Mistlands and the Ashlands) are drawn far off
  like your bases, once the game has made the area they stand in. Cave mouths and other parts the world keeps no record
  of are not.
- Fixed: with HD Terrain the far snow came out golden grey and the far Mistlands had brown patches where the moss should
  be. HD Terrain keeps its layers in an order of its own (the moss, the Swamp's mud, the snow as a colour, a normal map to
  each layer), and the far land now reads them so.

#### 1.1.3

- Fixed: with HD Terrain packs (Willybach's HD Valheim above all) the far land came out brown, dark and patchy, and the far
  Ashlands white. It now takes the colours of the near land. The far land looks a little different with Badger's pack too.

#### 1.1.2

- Changed: the first start in a world is much quicker. The far forest is made on several threads, so on a big world it
  no longer takes a minute to fill in (on a large custom map the first start went from 67 s to 25 s by FarLands' own count).
  The forest comes out the same.
- Added: a message in the top-left corner when FarLands has finished loading, after you enter a world or turn it on,
  with how long it took (the game's own messages, so mods that restyle them restyle it too). Off by default: turn it on
  with General -> LoadedMessage. The log says it either way.

### ValheimVisuals · `1.4.6` → `1.4.7`

<sub>by **Ivvty** · [Hexium](https://valheim.hexium.gg/mods/Ivvty/ValheimVisuals) · released 8 Nov 2026</sub>

- Reflections drop any broken (non-finite) pixel from each new render before showing it, so it can no longer appear as
  black marks on reflective surfaces, such as the dashes reported on the ice of the Deep North. Works with Smooth refresh
  on, the default.
- The grass radius (`Vegetation.UseDistance`, `Distance`) now applies only while SteadyFrame draws the grass on the
  GPU. Without it the game's own radius stays: there each 8 m patch of grass is a draw of its own, and 200 m cost some
  3.5 ms of main thread a frame more than the game's 45 m on open land (2560x1440), against 0.5-1 ms with SteadyFrame.
- With the radius applied, grass reaches it. Most kinds stopped at 100 m of their own whatever the radius, so 200 m
  looked like 100 m on the Plains. Reeds and water lilies stay near: they are heavy or cast shadows.
