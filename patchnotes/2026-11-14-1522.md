## Patch notes

### QuanPieces · `1.4.0` → `1.5.0`

<sub>by **Quandru** · [Thunderstore](https://thunderstore.io/c/valheim/p/Quandru/QuanPieces/) · released 14 Nov 2026</sub>

- New pieces: Quan's Wood Wall, Quan's Wood Wall Half and Quan's Wood Wall 1x1. The vanilla wood walls with only
  their vertical planks, no beams across the back, at vanilla's cost.
- New pieces: Quan's Wood Wall 2x3 (3 Wood) and Quan's Wood Wall 1x3 (2 Wood). Plank walls 3 m tall, with the wood
  grain carried up the planks rather than stretched. Like the walls above, they have no beams.
- New pieces: Quan's Triangular Wood Floor 1x1 (1) and (2), 1 Wood each. A 1x1 wood floor cut corner to corner,
  for small gaps or a neat 45° end to a 1x1 shelf. Mirror images of each other.
- New piece: Quan's Bevelled Shelf Edge, 1 Wood. The same triangle with its planks along the long edge, for a
  bevelled end to a 1x1 shelf.
- New pieces: Quan's Item Stand (V) and (H), for the wall and for the floor. Vanilla's item stands with only the
  nub, no backplate or underplate, so the item sits right against the wall or on the floor. Same cost as
  vanilla's.
- New pieces: Quan's Invisible Item Stand (V) and (H). The same, but the nub disappears while an item is on it.
