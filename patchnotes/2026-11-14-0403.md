### WeatherOverhaul · `1.0.5` → `1.0.6`

<sub>by **Ivvty** · [Hexium](https://valheim.hexium.gg/mods/Ivvty/WeatherOverhaul) · released 10 Nov 2026</sub>

- Clouds no longer darken the ground so much by day: the shade of a fair-weather cloud keeps about a third of the sunlight instead of a tenth. Overcast skies are unchanged.
- Taller clouds in fair weather: some cumulus now grow into tall rounded towers rising over the flat ones.
- No more fan of streaks from the sun across the high thin cloud layer.
- The small high puffy clouds (altocumulus) are smooth instead of grainy.
- Ashlands: no more large areas of open sky, only small gaps in the ash clouds.
