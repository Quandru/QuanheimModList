### MyDirtyHoe · `2.3.2` → `2.3.3`

<sub>by **MagicMike** · [Thunderstore](https://thunderstore.io/c/valheim/p/MagicMike/MyDirtyHoe/) · released 30 Oct 2026</sub>

- New Terrain Road added in the Terrain category, after the Terrain Ramp: click points along the way, then click the last point again to build. The road curves smoothly through all the points, and its height changes evenly from one point to the next. The preview shows the road's outline, length, width and steepest slope, turns red when a stretch is too steep or the road is too big, and yellow when a click will build it.
- Scroll the mouse wheel to change the road's width, or hold the Road Paint Modifier (default Left Shift) and scroll to choose its paint. The Step Back key (default Z) removes the last point, and a built road can be undone with the Undo Hotkey. With resource costs on, a road costs 10 Stone.
- The road uses the Max Slope, Min Width, Max Width, Shoulder Width and Max Vertices from the Ramp Settings. Its own settings are in the new Road Settings section.
- Road travel: sprinting on dirt is 10% faster and uses 10% less stamina, and on paved ground 20%. It counts any dirt or paved ground, not only roads built with the tool. Set Dirt Road Bonus and Paved Road Bonus to 0 to turn it off. On a server, the server's values are used.
- Settings are kept when config sections are renumbered: the new Road Settings section moves the sections after it along by one, and their settings are carried over from the old section names. Old leftover sections from earlier versions are cleaned out of the config file.

### QuanPieces · `1.1.0` → `1.2.0`

<sub>by **Quandru** · [Thunderstore](https://thunderstore.io/c/valheim/p/Quandru/QuanPieces/) · released 30 Oct 2026</sub>

- Added Quan's Planters, raised beds of cultivated soil in a wooden frame, in the QuanPieces tab:
  - **Planter**: 1x2, costs 10 Wood at a Workbench.
  - **Large Planter**: 1x4, costs 16 Wood at a Workbench.
- Anything you can plant with the cultivator grows in them, in any biome, under a roof, with no heat or cold
  damage, packed in as tightly as you like. Removing a planter gives back the seeds of anything still growing.
- Added Quan's Chicken Coop, three 2x2 pieces in the QuanPieces tab, each costing 2 Wood at a Workbench:
  - **Chicken Coop Floor**: a wooden floor with low thatch, so chicks stand tall. It gives off warmth, so eggs
    laid on it hatch.
  - **Chicken Coop Wall**: wooden bars you can see and reach through to pick up eggs, but animals can't pass.
  - **Chicken Coop Door**: a 2x2 gate of wooden bars with an iron latch.
